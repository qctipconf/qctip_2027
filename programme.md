# Outline

The QCTiP conference programme will bring together leading researchers and young scientists to share the latest developments and perspectives in the field of quantum computing. The programme will feature keynote talks by invited speakers, alongside contributed talks presenting recent research results. Dedicated poster sessions will provide an opportunity for informal discussions, exchange of ideas, and in-depth conversations with participants presenting their work.
